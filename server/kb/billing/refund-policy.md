# Refund Policy

Refunds are available within 30 days of the original charge for unused subscription periods and qualifying one-time purchases.

## Eligibility
- Full refund: cancellation within 14 days of first purchase with no usage beyond trial limits
- Partial refund: unused remaining days on annual plans after day 14
- No refund: consumed usage-based credits, third-party pass-through fees, or chargebacks already filed

## Agent steps
1. Verify purchase date, plan type, and whether a previous refund was issued.
2. Confirm the customer has not already received store credit for the same invoice.
3. For eligible full refunds, submit a refund request with invoice ID and reason code `customer_request`.
4. For partial refunds, calculate prorated amount from the billing portal before submitting.
5. Tell the customer refunds typically post in 5–10 business days depending on their bank.

## Exceptions
Enterprise contract refunds require manager approval. Never promise same-day bank posting.
